## Verified events (chronological)

### 2024-11-13 — First sighting at Picatinny Arsenal

- **Category:** observation
- **Narratives:** drones-legit
- **Evidence weight:** strong (reported consistently across multiple outlets)
- **Summary:** A contractor at Picatinny Arsenal in Morris County, NJ reported seeing "a light rising straight up from the tree line and toward the arsenal." This is the first confirmed observation in retrospective reporting.
- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_drone_sightings (footnote to NYT)
  - https://dronexl.co/2024/11/22/mysterious-drone-swarm-picatinny-arsenal-nj-multi-agency-investigation/
  - https://dronelife.com/2024/11/21/unexplained-drone-activity-over-morris-county-sparks-public-concern-and-official-investigation/
- **Notes:** Picatinny Arsenal Police Department later confirmed 11 reports of evening drone activity between November 13 and December 6 near the Wharton-based facility. Picatinny is a U.S. Army weapons research and development facility.

### 2024-11-18 — Additional Morris County sightings; law enforcement observations

- **Category:** observation
- **Narratives:** drones-legit
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** Additional unauthorized drones reported flying over the Picatinny area and surrounding Morris County. Local law enforcement reportedly observed drone activity firsthand.
- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_drone_sightings
  - https://www.yahoo.com/news/theyre-not-drones-says-picatinny-202450061.html
- **Notes:** Picatinny officials confirmed the sightings were not the result of activities related to the arsenal. Spokesperson told CNN: "The drone sightings are taking place well outside of hours when most of our workforce is at Picatinny."

### 2024-11-22 — FAA TFR over Trump's Bedminster golf club

- **Category:** policy-action
- **Narratives:** drones-legit
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** **[CORRECTION]** The original dossier dated this Dec 4. Wikipedia and contemporary reporting show FAA issued a two-week temporary flight restriction over Trump's Bedminster golf club starting around November 22, 2024, after observed drone activity in the area.
- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_drone_sightings (footnote 83)
- **Notes:** Bedminster TFR predates the broader December TFRs. JMill should pull the exact NOTAM from tfr.faa.gov for the canonical date and notice number.

### 2024-12-03 — FBI initiates investigation; requests public reports near Raritan River

- **Category:** policy-action
- **Narratives:** drones-legit
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** The FBI Newark field office formally initiated its investigation into the drone activity by requesting that the public report any sightings of suspected drones near the Raritan River corridor in New Jersey.
- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_drone_sightings
  - https://www.fbi.gov/contact-us/field-offices/newark/news/fbi-newark-seeks-information-on-drone-sightings
- **Notes:** This is the formal start of FBI involvement, distinct from the earlier informal sightings reporting.

### 2024-12-05 — NRC alert about lost radioactive medical equipment

- **Category:** institutional-statement
- **Narratives:** loose-nuke (origin point)
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission issued an alert that a piece of medical equipment used for cancer scans had been "lost in transit" in or near New Jersey. This unrelated NRC alert became the seed of the loose-nuke conspiracy theory when it was conflated with the drone sightings days later.
- **Sources:**
  - https://abcnews.com/US/fact-check-radioactive-material-triggered-drone-theories-missing/story?id=116884712
- **Notes:** The missing material was later found and disposed of properly. The mayor of Belleville, NJ publicly speculated that the drones were government-operated searching for the lost radioactive material; this speculation was the bridge from a real (mundane) NRC alert to the loose-nuke narrative.

### 2024-12-09 — White House National Security Communications Advisor John Kirby first addresses drone sightings

- **Category:** institutional-statement
- **Narratives:** mass-hysteria, drones-legit
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** John Kirby, NSC Communications Advisor at the Biden White House, first addressed the drone sightings at a White House press briefing. Kirby stated that federal agencies had no evidence the drones posed a threat to public safety or national security and characterized many sightings as misidentifications.
- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_drone_sightings
- **Notes:** Kirby gave multiple briefings on this topic in December. This is the first.

### 2024-12-10 — FBI tells Congress they have over 3,000 reports

- **Category:** institutional-statement
- **Narratives:** drones-legit
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** An FBI spokesperson briefed Congress that the bureau had collected over 3,000 reports via the tip line related to the drone sightings, but had not yet determined a full explanation. (This precedes the later 5,000 figure.)
- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_drone_sightings (footnote 66)
- **Notes:** The 3,000 → 5,000 progression is informative for the Information Vacuum Index calculation. The volume of public reporting was outpacing institutional ability to characterize.

### 2024-12-11 — NJ Assemblywoman Dawn Fantasia shares briefing notes

- **Category:** institutional-statement
- **Narratives:** drones-legit, uap
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** New Jersey State Assemblywoman Dawn Fantasia shared briefing notes describing observed drones as "up to six feet in diameter operating for six to seven hours per night." This characterization (large, long-endurance) became one of the strongest data points cited by those who rejected the hobbyist-drone framing.
- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_drone_sightings (footnote 31)
- **Notes:** A six-foot-diameter drone with six-to-seven-hour endurance is well beyond typical hobbyist class. This is the kind of detail that distinguishes the "uap" or "drones-legit but unusual" framing from the "mass-hysteria" framing.

### 2024-12-11 — Rep. Jeff Van Drew claims Iranian "mothership" on Fox News

- **Category:** narrative-claim
- **Narratives:** iranian-mothership
- **Evidence weight:** rumor (on the claim); strong (on the fact that he said it)
- **Summary:** Rep. Jeff Van Drew (R-NJ) appeared on Fox News with anchor Harris Faulkner and claimed that "from very high sources, very qualified sources, very responsible sources" Iran had launched a "mothership" approximately a month earlier off the U.S. East Coast that was launching drones over New Jersey. Van Drew also claimed Iran had made a deal with China to purchase drones, motherships, and technology. He called for the drones to be shot down.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.foxnews.com/media/new-jersey-lawmaker-warns-drones-originate-iranian-mothership-shot-down
  - https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2024/dec/11/jeff-van-drew-claims-new-jersey-drones-iranian-mot/
  - https://www.newsweek.com/drones-new-jersey-iran-jeff-van-drew-1999348
  - https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/iranian-mothership-not-drone-sightings-new-jersey-pentagon-says-rcna183863
- **Notes:** The canonical origin date of the "Iranian mothership" narrative. Van Drew has not fully retracted but acknowledged the claim was based on a single source he would not name.

### 2024-12-11 — Pentagon spokesperson Sabrina Singh denies Iranian mothership claim

- **Category:** institutional-statement
- **Narratives:** iranian-mothership (denial)
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** **[CORRECTION]** The original dossier said this was Pat Ryder. The Pentagon spokesperson who denied the claim was **Sabrina Singh**. Singh told reporters: "There is no Iranian ship off the coast of the United States, and there's no so-called 'mothership' launching drones toward the United States." Singh added there was no evidence the drones were linked to a foreign entity or adversary, that the drones were not from the U.S. military, and that the U.S. military had not shot down any drones since they did not pose a threat to military installations.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/iranian-mothership-not-drone-sightings-new-jersey-pentagon-says-rcna183863
  - https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2024-12-11/no-iranian-drone-mothership-off-the-united-states-launching-drones-pentagon-says
  - https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2024/12/11/new-jersey-drone-sightings/5751733955224/
- **Notes:** Same-day denial. The speed of the denial is itself notable for NVD calculation.

### 2024-12-13 — Stewart International Airport runway closure (1 hour)

- **Category:** policy-action
- **Narratives:** drones-legit
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** On the evening of December 13, 2024, Stewart International Airport (SWF) in Newburgh, NY closed its runways for approximately one hour due to drone activity in the airspace. The FAA confirmed slowed air traffic "due to multiple reported drone sightings near and over the airport." No safety impacts to aircraft were reported.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.cbsnews.com/newyork/news/drones-new-york-stewart-airfield/
  - https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/-gone-far-ny-gov-calls-federal-assistance-mysterious-drones-shut-airpo-rcna184256
  - https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/statement-governor-kathy-hochul-additional-drone-activity
- **Notes:** Governor Hochul issued a statement the following day (Dec 14): "Last night, the runways at Stewart Airfield were shut down for approximately one hour due to drone activity in the airspace. This has gone too far." She called for federal action and a drone detection system was subsequently deployed.

### 2024-12-16 — Joint DoD/DHS/FAA/FBI statement: "over 5,000 sightings, nothing anomalous"

- **Category:** institutional-statement
- **Narratives:** drones-legit, mass-hysteria
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** A joint statement from the Department of Defense, DHS, FAA, and FBI concluded their investigation reviewing "over 5,000 reported sightings" and found "nothing anomalous" and "no national security or public safety risk." ### 2024-12-17 — Laser strikes on aircraft surge in New Jersey

- **Category:** evidence
- **Narratives:** drones-legit (consequence)
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** The FAA reported a 269% increase in laser strikes on aircraft in New Jersey in December 2024 compared to the same period in 2023. Specifically, 59 laser incidents were reported between December 1 and 16, vs. 16 over the same period the previous year. On December 17 alone, 123 laser strikes were reported by pilots nationwide, with 24 of those over New Jersey. Pilots were being struck in the eyes by ground-based individuals who believed they were targeting unmanned drones.
- **Sources:**
  - https://abcnews.go.com/US/new-jersey-drones-laser-strikes-pilots-faa/story?id=116926439
  - https://www.cnn.com/2024/12/18/us/faa-laser-strikes-drone-mystery/index.html
  - https://nj1015.com/drones-linked-surge-laser-strikes/
- **Notes:** A helicopter pilot was struck in the eyes by a laser and required treatment upon landing in one December incident. This is a real public-safety consequence of the information vacuum and a useful Sensemaking Quality Metric input.

## Cross-cutting findings

### AARO position throughout the incident

AARO (the All-Domain Anomaly Resolution Office) stated it "has received no reports of UAP in conjunction with the recent drone flights or incursions in New Jersey and other eastern states" and was prepared to support law enforcement agencies if requested. AARO chief Jon Kosloski told lawmakers that AARO supports other federal agencies "through an advisory capacity" when incursions by seemingly unexplainable technology occur.

- Source: https://defensescoop.com/2024/12/16/new-jersey-drones-federal-assessments-suggest-not-uap-or-us-military-assets/

**Sensemaking note:** AARO's choice to classify the events as UAS rather than UAP is itself a categorization decision worth tracking in the H1-H5 framework. The choice prejudges the hypothesis space.

### Ryan Graves / Americans for Safe Aerospace position

Ryan Graves, founder of Americans for Safe Aerospace (where JMill volunteers), stated the New Jersey drones were "one-hundred percent" a national security threat, demonstrating "our lack of coordination, our lack of ability to detect small form factors that are flying at low altitudes, and our inability to respond to it in a timely and effective manner." Graves articulated a three-step response framework: detect, identify, respond.

- Source: https://thenationaldesk.com/news/americas-news-now/mystery-drones-over-jersey-were-absolutely-witnessing-a-national-security-gap-uap-unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-ufo-airspace-security-americans-for-safe-aerospace

**Sensemaking note:** Graves' three-step framework (detect, identify, respond) maps directly onto the CU26 paper's Detect → Identify pipeline architecture and onto the praxis itself. The pipeline being built for TMP IS a computational instantiation of what Graves was publicly arguing for in real time during this incident.
