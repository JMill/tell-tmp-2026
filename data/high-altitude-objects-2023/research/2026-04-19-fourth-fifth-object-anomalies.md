**Compiled:** 2026-04-19

## Why a dedicated anomalies dossier

Three of the four kinetic engagements in the February 2023 cluster produced objects that were never physically recovered. The eight-day sequence collapses to a single physical-evidence data point (the PRC balloon) surrounded by three observational records without debris attribution. The fourth object (Lake Huron, February 12) and the fifth episode (Montana radar anomaly, February 11) sit at the extreme end of the attribution-uncertainty spectrum: one destroyed at low altitude with two missiles and no recovery, and one never visually acquired at all.

User-stated interest focuses on these two episodes because they are where the `official-recreation-research`, `hobbyist-pico-balloon`, `uap-unknown`, and `misidentification-threshold` narratives compete most directly. From a Sensemaking Quality Metrics perspective, they are also where the contradicting-evidence-lag and hypothesis-entropy signals should behave most informatively.

## Anomaly one: Lake Huron object (February 12, 2023)

### Observable kinematics as disclosed

- First NORAD radar acquisition: approximately 16:45 EST, February 11, 2023, in Canadian airspace roughly 70 miles north of the US border.
- Engagement time: approximately 14:42 EST, February 12, 2023.
- Engagement altitude: approximately 20,000 feet. This is well below the PRC balloon (~60,000-65,000 ft), Alaska object (~40,000 ft), and Yukon object (~40,000 ft) engagement altitudes.
- Engagement location: over Lake Huron, on the US side of the US-Canada border.
- Engaging aircraft: F-16 Fighting Falcon from the 148th Fighter Wing, Minnesota Air National Guard.
- Munitions expended: two AIM-9X Sidewinder short-range air-to-air missiles. The first missed and impacted the lake surface. The second struck the object.
- Object characteristics as disclosed: octagonal shape with "strings dangling from it" per NORAD and Pentagon statements.

Sources: https://www.war.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/3296905/efforts-underway-to-recover-object-downed-over-lake-huron/, https://www.military.com/daily-news/2023/02/14/f-16-missile-missed-object-shot-down-over-lake-huron-joint-chiefs-chairman-says.html, https://theaviationist.com/2023/02/12/f-16-shoots-down-an-unidentified-octagon-shaped-object-over-lake-huron/.

### Why the engagement is anomalous

1. **Missile miss.** The first AIM-9X miss is the only publicly-disclosed US-fighter miss against a live target since 1978. The AIM-9X has an infrared seeker optimized for heat-emitting targets; an unpowered balloon at cold ambient temperature presents a near-zero heat signature. The miss is consistent with an extremely cold, slow-moving target outside the seeker's training envelope.

2. **Engagement altitude.** At 20,000 feet, the object was low enough to pose an aviation-collision risk that the higher-altitude objects did not. This justified the shootdown decision under existing rules of engagement. It also means the object had been descending or had been low from first detection. Neither trajectory is consistent with a typical PRC ISR balloon, which operates at 60,000+ feet for line-of-sight intelligence advantages.

3. **Recovery failure.** The object fell into Lake Huron in shallow water. The search was conducted by the US Coast Guard with Canadian cooperation. No wreckage was located. The search was formally abandoned after several days. Lake Huron's average depth is roughly 195 feet and its floor is largely visible to sonar; the failure to recover is hard to explain if the object was solid.

4. **Octagonal-with-strings description.** This description is visually consistent with a small commercial or hobbyist balloon with a suspended payload bundle. It is not consistent with a purpose-built ISR balloon, which is typically spherical or teardrop-shaped with an integrated gondola. The descriptive language points toward the `hobbyist-pico-balloon` or `official-recreation-research` narratives rather than the `prc-surveillance` or `classified-us-tech` narratives.

### Hypothesis scoring against H1-H5

- **H1 (Artifact):** low. The object was observed by multiple sensors (NORAD radar, F-16 targeting systems, visual acquisition by F-16 pilots) across several hours. The Lake Huron object was not a sensor artifact.
- **H2 (Natural):** low. No natural phenomenon matches an octagonal shape with strings dangling.
- **H3 (Unclassified Technology):** high. A small commercial, scientific, or hobbyist balloon with a suspended payload fits every disclosed observable. The `hobbyist-pico-balloon` and `official-recreation-research` narratives are both H3.
- **H4 (Classified Technology):** low to moderate. A US-classified test asset cannot be fully excluded given the anomalous engagement characteristics, but no US-government admission has occurred. Adversary attribution (`prc-surveillance`, `adversary-black-tech`) is implausible given the low altitude, octagonal description, and absence of technical capability markers in the engagement.
- **H5 (Unknown):** low. The observables are consistent enough with H3 that a residual-unknown claim is not supported.

Priors shift the distribution toward H3 sharply.

### Open questions

1. Has any US government agency reviewed the NIBBB K9YO-15 WSPR telemetry against the F-16 engagement geometry on the day of the Lake Huron shootdown? Public disclosure has been silent on cross-referencing hobbyist-balloon telemetry against engagement tracks.
2. Does the FBI's Quantico Laboratory Division retain any recovered fragments from the Lake Huron engagement, or has the recovery-failure finding been final since February 2023?
3. What was the seeker-head post-engagement data on the AIM-9X first-shot miss? A formal seeker-head analysis would clarify whether the miss was due to target thermal signature (supporting a cold-hobbyist-balloon attribution) or to guidance failure.

## Anomaly two: Montana radar anomaly (February 11, 2023)

### Observable kinematics as disclosed

- Initial radar detection: evening of February 11, 2023, in airspace over north-central Montana near Havre.
- FAA response: temporary flight restriction issued; airspace closed.
- Military response: F-15 fighters scrambled to investigate.
- Visual acquisition: none. Pilots reported no visual or targeting-radar confirmation of any physical object.
- NORAD subsequent characterization: "radar anomaly." The airspace was reopened after several hours.
- Recovery: none attempted. No object to recover.

Sources: https://www.ktvq.com/object-hovering-above-montana-airspace-causes-faa-to-issue-temporary-flight-restriction, https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2023/feb/11/faa-temporarily-closed-montana-airspace-after-ufo-/, https://www.aerotime.aero/articles/war-on-uaps-restrictions-over-montana-triggered-by-radar-anomaly-us-military.

### Why the episode is anomalous

1. **No object to explain.** The Montana event is the only one of the five where the fundamental observable is not an object but a radar signature. Every narrative in `narratives.yaml` other than `misidentification-threshold` presumes a physical object existed; Montana is the case where that presumption fails.

2. **Full operational response.** The FAA issued a temporary flight restriction and F-15s scrambled. Operationally, the US government treated the radar signature as a real object for several hours. The subsequent re-characterization as "anomaly" does not negate that the response was initially full.

3. **Narrative survival.** The Montana episode is still widely treated as the fifth in an eight-day sequence despite the absence of a confirmed object. This is precisely the kind of narrative persistence that the Source Independence Graph and Narrative Velocity Divergence signals are designed to detect. The episode's continued inclusion in popular sequencing reflects a narrative fit, not an observational basis.

### Connection to the NORAD radar-gate adjustment

Gen. Glen VanHerck disclosed on February 12-13 that NORAD had narrowed its velocity and altitude filters after the February 4 PRC balloon shootdown. The adjustment had the intended effect of making NORAD sensitive to slow-moving, low-radar-cross-section objects. It also had the predictable secondary effect of admitting more false positives into the tracking system.

The Montana radar anomaly is the most likely observable consequence of that adjustment. If a filter that normally excludes low-velocity, low-signal returns is loosened, more such returns will be generated, and a fraction of those returns will be spurious: clutter, atmospheric ducting, multipath, sidelobes. Each of these is itself a listed H1 sub-hypothesis in `dev/tell/data/hypotheses.yaml`.

Sources: https://defensescoop.com/2023/02/13/norad-adjusts-radar-gates-to-sharpen-detection-of-anomalous-objects-as-ufo-recovery-intensifies/, https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/national-security/powerful-radars-limited-scope-no-one-spotted-bunch-objects-floating-no-rcna70690.

### Hypothesis scoring against H1-H5

- **H1 (Artifact):** high. This is the exemplar case for the `misidentification-threshold` narrative. Parallax, multipath, calibration drift, and sidelobe returns are all H1 sub-hypotheses and are individually plausible after a sensitivity adjustment.
- **H2 (Natural):** low to moderate. Atmospheric ducting or thermal inversion can produce real radar signatures without a physical target. Montana in February has thermal inversions frequently.
- **H3 through H5:** low by default, because no object was confirmed. Any scoring here depends on accepting the presumption that an object existed, which is precisely what the observables do not support.

Priors heavily favor H1, with H2 as a distant second.

### Open questions

1. Has NORAD released a post-hoc analysis of the specific radar returns that triggered the Havre TFR? A formal technical release would close the `misidentification-threshold` attribution loop.
2. Were any civilian sensors (FAA radar, ADS-B, commercial aviation weather radar) able to corroborate or refute the NORAD returns in real time?
3. How many radar anomalies did the adjusted NORAD gates produce in the week after February 4, and why was Montana the only one that triggered an operational response? A count of unpublicized spurious tracks would quantify the false-positive rate introduced by the sensitivity adjustment.

## Combined analysis: what the anomalies tell us about the pipeline

The Lake Huron and Montana episodes sit on opposite ends of the physical-evidence spectrum within the same anomalous-objects phase. Lake Huron is the most "maximal" of the unrecovered objects: real object, real shootdown, no wreckage. Montana is the most "minimal": no object, no shootdown, full operational response.

For the TELL pipeline:

1. **IVI behavior.** Information Vacuum Index should peak sharply during the February 10-14 window. Public discourse on four visible objects in eight days should run well ahead of institutional attribution, which did not issue until Kirby's February 14 "recreation, commercial, or research" framing. The Lake Huron and Montana episodes drive most of that discourse-to-response gap.

2. **NVD behavior.** Narrative Velocity Divergence should fire most strongly on the `hobbyist-pico-balloon` narrative on February 17 (NIBBB statement) and on the `misidentification-threshold` narrative on February 13 (VanHerck disclosure). Both narratives arise after the events they explain, which means their velocity-relative-to-evidence should look anomalous.

3. **SIG behavior.** Source Independence Graph should show the `official-recreation-research` narrative originating from a single node (John Kirby, February 14 briefing) and amplifying into many downstream documents without substantive corroboration. The `hobbyist-pico-balloon` narrative should show a small number of independent nodes (NIBBB, WSPR operators, a handful of amateur-radio forums) with modest amplification.

4. **SQM behavior.** Contradicting-evidence-lag for the `official-recreation-research` narrative is especially short and especially damning: the attribution was offered before evidence was available, and the NIBBB statement three days later provides the first grassroots-technical corroboration rather than the government being able to point to corroborating evidence of its own.

The pipeline's empirical value in this case is not a definitive attribution of the Lake Huron or Yukon objects. It is a reliable characterization of how narrative formation outran evidence formation, and of which narratives survived on information-vacuum fuel versus which survived on independent-source corroboration.
