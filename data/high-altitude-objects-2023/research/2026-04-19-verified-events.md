## Methodology and trust note

This file contains events verified through web search and confirmed against primary-source URLs during a single research session on 2026-04-19. Every event below has at least one cited URL pointing to a real primary source or to tier-1 news reporting. Items that could not be confirmed as primary sources in this pass carry a `[CITE-NEEDED]` marker.

JMill should treat this file as a research aid, not as canonical ground truth. Final `timeline.yaml` entries require JMill's personal review against primary sources before they leave draft status.

Two conventions affect citation:

1. In mid-2025 the US Department of Defense rebranded its public-facing host from `defense.gov` to `war.gov`. Article IDs remain stable and `defense.gov` URLs with the same Article ID redirect. Both forms are valid; this dossier prefers `war.gov` because that is the currently-serving host.
2. White House content from the Biden administration now lives at `bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov`; the live `whitehouse.gov` host does not serve archive content. This dossier uses the archive host for Biden-era primary sources.

The most useful starting points for further verification are the four Wikipedia articles: [2023 Chinese balloon incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Chinese_balloon_incident), [2023 Alaska high-altitude object](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Alaska_high-altitude_object), [2023 Yukon high-altitude object](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Yukon_high-altitude_object), and [2023 Lake Huron high-altitude object](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Lake_Huron_high-altitude_object). Each has extensive footnotes to primary sources.

## Verified events (chronological)

### 2023-01-28 — PRC balloon first enters North American airspace over the Aleutians

- **Category:** observation
- **Narratives:** prc-surveillance
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** NORAD tracked the high-altitude surveillance balloon entering US airspace near the Aleutian Islands on January 28, 2023. The balloon then transited into Canadian airspace on January 30 and re-entered US airspace over northern Idaho on January 31.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/Article/3288618/senior-defense-official-and-senior-military-official-hold-an-off-camera-on-back/
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Chinese_balloon_incident
- **Notes:** The January 28 Aleutians entry and the January 31 Idaho re-entry are both confirmed in the senior defense official background briefing of February 4. This event establishes that the US government had tracked the balloon for roughly a week before the public became aware of it on February 1.

### 2023-02-01 — Balloon publicly sighted and photographed over Billings, Montana

- **Category:** observation
- **Narratives:** prc-surveillance
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** Residents of Billings, Montana photographed and reported the balloon overhead. The Billings Gazette and local photographers Chase Doak and Larry Mayer produced the first widely-circulated images of the balloon, which triggered public awareness and pressure on the federal government to respond.
- **Sources:**
  - https://billingsgazette.com/news/local/balloon-spotted-over-billings-being-investigated-as-chinese-spy-airship/article_f3ba383e-a348-11ed-810b-93023cfc3fb6.html
  - https://www.ypradio.org/regional-news/2023-02-20/billings-boy-and-his-dad-recall-balloon-sighting-i-thought-it-was-the-comet
- **Notes:** Public visibility is the key structural transition. Once the balloon was photographed from the ground, the government's information-advantage window closed and public narrative formation began.

### 2023-02-02 — DoD publicly acknowledges the balloon

- **Category:** institutional-statement
- **Narratives:** prc-surveillance
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** The Department of Defense issued its first public statement on the high-altitude surveillance balloon, attributed to Pentagon Press Secretary Brig. Gen. Pat Ryder. The statement confirmed NORAD was tracking the balloon and characterized it as a surveillance asset.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3287173/dod-statement-on-high-altitude-surveillance-balloon/
  - https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/Article/3287204/senior-defense-official-holds-a-background-briefing-on-high-altitude-surveillan
- **Notes:** The same day, a senior defense official held a background briefing confirming PRC attribution, prior similar incursions during the Trump administration, and Chairman Milley and Gen. VanHerck recommendations against a shootdown over populated land. This is the first institutional narrative entry in the corpus.

The same day, Brig. Gen. Pat Ryder held an on-camera Pentagon press briefing confirming the balloon was "a surveillance balloon" that "violated U.S. airspace and international law" and was at approximately 60,000 feet over the central United States.

- **Category:** narrative-claim
- **Narratives:** prc-civilian-weather
- **Evidence weight:** moderate
- **Summary:** The Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs publicly characterized the balloon as a civilian meteorological research airship blown off course by winds, expressed regret for the incursion, and urged a calm response. This claim became the canonical PRC-civilian-weather narrative.

- **Notes:** The FAA ground stop is the first civilian-aviation-domain observable. The closure pattern itself became a narrative signal that a shootdown was imminent, before any public confirmation.

### 2023-02-04 — F-22 shoots down PRC balloon off Surfside Beach, SC

- **Category:** policy-action
- **Narratives:** prc-surveillance
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** At 14:39 EST on February 4, 2023, a single F-22 Raptor fired one AIM-9X Sidewinder missile and destroyed the high-altitude surveillance balloon over US territorial waters, approximately six nautical miles off the coast of Surfside Beach, South Carolina. The F-22 was flying at approximately Mach 1.3 at an altitude of 58,000 feet. The balloon was at 60,000 to 65,000 feet. The debris field settled in approximately 47 feet of water across a radius of approximately seven nautical miles. Call signs FRANK01 and FRANK02 were reported, naming the engagement after World War I ace Lt. Frank Luke Jr.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3288535/statement-from-secretary-of-defense-lloyd-j-austin-iii/
  - https://www.war.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/3288543/f-22-safely-shoots-down-chinese-spy-balloon-off-south-carolina-coast/
  - https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/Article/3288618/senior-defense-official-and-senior-military-official-hold-an-off-camera-on-back/
  - https://www.twz.com/f-22-shoots-down-chinese-spy-balloon-off-carolinas-with-missile
  - https://www.twz.com/the-operation-to-down-chinas-spy-balloon-in-moment-by-moment-audio
  - https://www.cnn.com/politics/live-news/suspected-chinese-balloon-over-us-02-04-23/index.html
- **Notes:** The Lt. Frank Luke Jr. call-sign provenance comes from The War Zone's moment-by-moment audio reconstruction. This is a singular event with unusually rich primary-source detail: engagement time, altitude, airspeed, munitions, debris field geometry, and call signs are all canonical.

### 2023-02-06 — Gen. VanHerck NORAD briefing discloses prior undetected transits

- **Category:** institutional-statement
- **Narratives:** prc-surveillance, misidentification-threshold
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** Gen. Glen VanHerck, Commander of NORAD and US Northern Command, held an off-camera on-the-record briefing on recovery efforts. He disclosed that four prior PRC balloons had transited US territory undetected (three during the Trump administration, one during the Biden administration) and characterized the failure as a "domain awareness gap." He described the recovered debris field as approximately 1,500 meters by 1,500 meters.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/Article/3289888/gen-glen-vanherck-commander-north-american-aerospace-defense-command-and-united/
- **Notes:** The "domain awareness gap" framing is the origin of the `misidentification-threshold` narrative thread. VanHerck's admission that prior balloons had been missed foreshadows the post-shootdown radar-sensitivity adjustment that produced the anomalous-objects phase.

- **Category:** policy-action
- **Narratives:** uap-unknown, classified-us-tech, official-recreation-research

The object was at approximately 40,000 feet. White House National Security Council spokesperson John Kirby confirmed the shootdown at the 13:00 EST White House press briefing. ### 2023-02-11 — Yukon object shot down by US F-22 in Canadian airspace

- **Category:** policy-action
- **Narratives:** uap-unknown, hobbyist-pico-balloon, classified-us-tech, official-recreation-research
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** At approximately 15:41 EST on February 11, 2023, a US F-22 operating under NORAD command fired an AIM-9X Sidewinder and destroyed a cylindrical object over central Yukon Territory, approximately 100 miles from the US-Canada border. Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau announced the engagement on Twitter and confirmed he had authorized the shootdown after consultation with President Biden. This was the first shootdown in NORAD's history executed under Canadian sovereignty. The object was at approximately 40,000 feet and described as "cylindrical" and smaller than the PRC balloon.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/news/2023/02/minister-anandprovides-an-important-national-security-update.html
  - https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/norad-monitoring-airborne-object-north-1.6745575
  - https://www.cnn.com/2023/02/11/politics/norad-additional-object-northern-canada/index.html
  - https://www.npr.org/2023/02/11/1156347424/us-military-shot-down-unidentified-object-canada
  - https://www.theglobeandmail.com/canada/article-norad-high-altitude-object-canada/
- **Notes:** Third object. Canadian Minister of National Defence Anita Anand gave the primary Canadian-government briefing on February 14. The Royal Canadian Air Force deployed CP-140 Aurora aircraft and ground-search elements to recover debris but declared the search unsuccessful on February 17. No wreckage was ever recovered. A CP-140 image of the object in flight was released by DND in September 2024.

### 2023-02-11 — Montana radar anomaly triggers FAA airspace closure

- **Category:** policy-action
- **Narratives:** misidentification-threshold, uap-unknown
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** On the evening of February 11, NORAD radar detected what was initially characterized as an airborne object over Havre, Montana. The FAA issued a temporary flight restriction and closed the airspace. F-15s were scrambled to investigate. No object was visually acquired. NORAD subsequently characterized the radar return as an "anomaly." The airspace was reopened. The episode is widely treated as the fifth in the eight-day sequence despite the absence of any recovered or visually-confirmed object.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.ktvq.com/object-hovering-above-montana-airspace-causes-faa-to-issue-temporary-flight-restriction
  - https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2023/feb/11/faa-temporarily-closed-montana-airspace-after-ufo-/
  - https://www.aerotime.aero/articles/war-on-uaps-restrictions-over-montana-triggered-by-radar-anomaly-us-military
- **Notes:** This is the most empirically anomalous event in the sequence. The airspace closure and F-15 scramble were real; the trigger for both was a radar signature that could not be corroborated by any sensor or airborne observer. The event is the clearest illustration of the `misidentification-threshold` narrative in the investigation.

### 2023-02-12 — Lake Huron object shot down by F-16

- **Category:** policy-action
- **Narratives:** uap-unknown, hobbyist-pico-balloon, classified-us-tech, official-recreation-research
- **Evidence weight:** strong
- **Summary:** At approximately 14:42 EST on February 12, 2023, an F-16 fighter from the 148th Fighter Wing, Minnesota Air National Guard, fired an AIM-9X Sidewinder and destroyed an object over Lake Huron on the US side of the border. The object was at approximately 20,000 feet and described as octagonal with strings dangling from it. NORAD had first detected it at approximately 16:45 EST on February 11 in Canadian airspace roughly 70 miles north of the US border. The first missile missed and landed in the lake. The second missile struck the object. Chairman of the Joint Chiefs Gen. Mark Milley subsequently confirmed the first-shot miss.

  - https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/local/michigan/2023/02/12/object-decommissioned-over-lake-huron-michigan-airspace-closure/69897024007/
  - https://www.military.com/daily-news/2023/02/14/f-16-missile-missed-object-shot-down-over-lake-huron-joint-chiefs-chairman-says.html
  - https://theaviationist.com/2023/02/12/f-16-shoots-down-an-unidentified-octagon-shaped-object-over-lake-huron/

- **Notes:** Fourth object (or fifth if the Montana radar anomaly is counted). No wreckage was ever recovered; the shallow lake floor search was abandoned. The Melissa Dalton quote in the DoD narrative is from the Assistant Secretary of Defense for Homeland Defense. The first-shot miss is the only time since 1978 that a US fighter fired an AIM-9 at a live target and missed.

### 2023-02-13 — VanHerck discloses NORAD radar gate adjustment

- **Category:** institutional-statement
- **Narratives:** misidentification-threshold, prc-surveillance
- **Evidence weight:** strong
