The instrument

Three measures catch three distinct failure modes

IVI
The gap between public discourse and institutional response. Information Vacuum Index · rises when people talk and institutions go quiet
NVD
How fast a claim spreads against how fast its evidence accumulates. Narrative Velocity Divergence · popular and unsupported is a measurable state
SIG
Whether repetition is corroboration or echo. Source Independence Graph · counts independent origins, not outlets
